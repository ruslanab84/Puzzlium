Puzzlium Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Puzzlium is a jigsaw puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. Puzzlium has no accounts and no servers of its own.

Data that stays on your device

  • Game progress (solved puzzles, saved games, streaks, settings) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Your photos. If you add a picture from your photo library or take one with the camera, it is used only to make a puzzle and is kept only on your device. Puzzlium never uploads your photos. The app asks for camera access only when you choose “Take a photo”; photo library pictures are chosen with the system picker, which gives the app only the picture you select.
  • Generated pictures. On devices with Apple Intelligence, pictures made with Image Playground are created by Apple on your device and kept only in Puzzlium.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Puzzlium never sees your payment details; it only receives confirmation from the App Store of which items you own. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The free version shows ads provided by Unity Technologies (“Unity Ads”). To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Unity Ads may collect:

  • device identifiers for vendors (IDFV) and device information (model, OS version, language, screen size);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions (ads shown, viewed, tapped) and diagnostic data.

Puzzlium does not ask for permission to track you, so the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) is not shared with Unity. Puzzlium also tells Unity that you have not given consent to personalized advertising, so in the EU, EEA and UK you see contextual (non-personalized) ads only. Unity processes this data under its own policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, where you can also find how to opt out and exercise your rights.

Buying “Remove ads” or “All packs” turns banner and full-screen ads off. Watching an optional rewarded ad is always your choice.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not run our own analytics servers.

Children

Puzzlium is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete data about you. Data stored by Puzzlium itself is only on your device and is removed when you delete the app. For data held by Unity, use the options in Unity’s policy above or contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
